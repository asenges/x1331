INTERPRETATION RULE
-------------------
A fingerprint family is informative only if
it improves OUT-OF-SAMPLE walk-forward
performance over the historical PRIOR.

R@K alone is insufficient.
Lower LogLoss/Brier is stronger evidence
that the conditional probabilities improved.

MERKLE/PREVHASH associations do NOT imply
cryptographic SHA predictability.
They may identify mining systems, epochs,
pool construction, or other process effects.

This experiment predicts HISTORICAL winning
nonce geometry, not counterfactual SHA success.
